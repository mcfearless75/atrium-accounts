Getting off Sage — the order to do it in

  1. Export everything out of Sage first
    Customers, suppliers, products, nominal codes and trial balance, and the full audit trail. Do this while the licence is live — once a subscription lapses the data gets very hard to retrieve. Archive the raw files somewhere safe before touching anything else.
  2. See what state the data is actually in
    Run the audit trail through the Ledger module. You will know within a minute whether you are migrating clean books or fixing problems first — and the findings are worth raising with the client either way.
  3. Convert the exports
    Drop each export into Migration. Every defect is flagged before conversion, then you get ERPNext-ready import files back, including a chart of accounts and a balanced opening journal.
  4. Reconcile weekly through the parallel run
    Same period from both systems into the Reconcile tab. Investigate every difference until a report comes back clean — that clean report is what the accountant signs off before cutover.
No server, no accounts, no telemetry. Every module parses and analyses in your browser; nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored beyond your own preferences. Safe for live client data.
